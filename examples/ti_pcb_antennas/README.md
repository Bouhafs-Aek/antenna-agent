## What is in each KiCad file
- Copper as zones with nets RF / GND (DN024, SWRA730) or as unnetted graphic copper polygons (AN043, DN023: the shorted leg ties RF and GND together in copper, so nets would be false).
- Lumped-port footprint `PORT_P1` (pad 1 RF on F.Cu, pad 2 GND): across the 1 mm gap to the ground edge (DN023, DN024), 1.4 mm (SWRA730), or on the feed pad over ground layer 2 (AN043).
- Edge.Cuts outline, stackup thickness in the file header, a note on the Cmts.User layer.

## Assumptions to check (not in the notes or read from a figure)
- AN043: PCB thickness, ground size, via position/size (0.6 / 0.3 mm placeholder under the via pad).
- DN023 / DN024 / SWRA730: ground-plane size below the antenna is a placeholder except DN023 (31 x 45) and DN024 (63 x 43, from the note). The CPW feed pocket and the pi/LC match (Z60-Z64) are not drawn; place the parts from the note.
- Bottom-layer copies of DN024 / SWRA730 are joined to the top by 2 placeholder vias (positions are my guess: the notes say only that the antenna is on both layers).
- Spacings in DN023's meander read from the figure at about +-0.5 mm; pitch 5.0 mm between legs, leg width 1.0 mm.
- None of these files was opened in KiCad or simulated here.
